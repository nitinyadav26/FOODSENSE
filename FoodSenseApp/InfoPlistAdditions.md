Add the following keys to your Info.plist:

- NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription: "FoodSense needs Bluetooth to connect to your smart food scale."
- NSCameraUsageDescription: "FoodSense uses the camera to capture your meals for nutrition analysis."
- NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription: "FoodSense can optionally save meal photos to your photo library."

Set the minimum deployment target to iOS 17.0.
